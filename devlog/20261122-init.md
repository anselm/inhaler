Claude (Fable 5.2, working from the esimv1 room on canada1, session
https://claude.ai/code/session_018TGCFiQHbUYn12HkNti2xk; that session's logs are under
`~/.claude/projects/-home-anselm-projects-2026-earth-simulator-1/`).

## The ask

Anselm, first, while the esimv1 work of the day was going on:

> for cortes.exe.xyz and saltspring.exe.xyz i attempted to collect all the general ambient
> news and media and sources i possibly could - to make a rich data layer of social
> information over those areas, even radio stations. this information layer feels
> orthogonal to the rest - but honestly it could also provide some fuel for discovery of
> issues we may want to model - i could see an llm constantly reading this and using it to
> make decisions. we may want to think about a way to have a more or less independent
> module or service that can do this for arbitrary places - and have a social information
> layer like this in the user experience as well.

Then:

> create a esimv1-social . feel free to do your own fresh thinking. but note that we did
> think this through a couple of times already. there may be useful thinking from the
> cortes and saltspring repos about how to write an aggregator, back off and so on. we
> will need to digest what we consume, i think a multi-pass approach is best. we can
> collect facts, geolocate them and so on - it doesn't have to deal with presentation - i
> just want to have effectively a smart aggregator that can inhale information. note that
> we can use this api : https://api.fxtwitter.com - twitter was an barrier before . the
> source code for cortes and saltspring should be visible to you. worth noting that our
> matchmaker and our ontology project _also_ do this. so we have quite a few projects that
> are inhaling content . i wonder if we should have a more general inhaler and then have
> those other projects merely talk to this inhaler?

## What it rests on

- Four earlier projects of his, read (not changed) by two reading agents on this day:
  `~/projects/2026/cortes`, `~/projects/2026/saltspring`, and shallow clones of
  `anselm/matchmaker` and `anselm/ontology-scraper`. What was taken from each is in
  `20261122-design.md`.
- The house policy on aggregation (the harness's modular-architecture skill): sources
  behind one interface and configured, fetch and analysis as separate phases, hashing,
  gone-marking, backoff with a cap and jitter, manners.
- The research on Sausalito done by hand earlier the same day in
  `esimv1-playable-sausalito` (`devlog/20261122-sausalito-from-primary-pages.md`), which is
  the job this program is meant to do continually.
